Bank Transcript Pipeline

How 679 raw PDF earnings-call transcripts become a validated, bank-by-bank signal of what is being said and how it sounds.

Main goal

Turn unstructured bank earnings-call PDFs into a validated, quantified view of what each bank talks about and the tone it uses — evidence a data science team can turn into business recommendations for the Bank of England, not just raw model output.

INPUT

Where it starts

679 official PDF earnings-call transcripts from JPMorgan and Citigroup, spanning several years of quarters — unstructured text, no labels, no topics, no sentiment.

OUTPUT

Where it ends

A written report, code, slide deck and a 15-minute live presentation built on a validated table of sentiment and topic per bank per quarter — the evidence base for the business recommendations.
